On December 17, 2009, organization called Syndicate was built around the CS:GO team.
It is noteworthy that initially the abbreviation Syndicate was borrowed from the film "Avatar",
and its final form was acquired after the competition for the team name, conducted among the fans.
The first Syndicate lineup included Edward, markeloff, starix, ceh9 and Zeus, with ZeroGravity becoming
the manager. All the players were distinguished by high individual skill and had a lot of experience
in tournaments.
